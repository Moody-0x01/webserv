Refreshing Cold Drinks

Easy, beat-the-heat recipes to cool you down.

Classic Iced Lemonade

Ingredients

  • 1 cup freshly squeezed lemon juice (4-6 lemons)
  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • 6 cups cold water
  • Ice cubes
  • Lemon slices & mint (for garnish)

Instructions

  1. In a small saucepan, combine the sugar and 1 cup of water. Heat gently, stirring until the sugar dissolves completely to make a simple syrup. Let it cool.
  2. In a large pitcher, combine the lemon juice, the cooled simple syrup, and the remaining 5 cups of cold water.
  3. Stir well and refrigerate until perfectly chilled.
  4. Serve over ice, garnished with lemon slices and fresh mint.

Perfect Cold Brew Coffee

Ingredients

  • 1 cup coarse ground coffee beans
  • 4 cups cold filtered water
  • Ice cubes
  • Milk or cream (optional)
  • Simple syrup (optional)

Instructions

  1. Place the coarse coffee grounds in a large glass jar or pitcher.
  2. Pour the cold water over the grounds and stir gently to ensure all grounds are completely saturated.
  3. Cover the jar and let it steep in the refrigerator for 12 to 24 hours (18 hours is usually the sweet spot).
  4. Strain the mixture through a fine-mesh sieve lined with a coffee filter or cheesecloth to remove all grounds.
  5. Serve the cold brew concentrate over ice, diluted with a splash of water or milk to taste.

Watermelon Mint Slushie

Ingredients

  • 4 cups diced seedless watermelon (frozen overnight)
  • 1 tbsp fresh lime juice
  • 5-6 fresh mint leaves
  • 1 tbsp honey or agave (optional)
  • Splash of water or coconut water

Instructions

  1. Place the frozen diced watermelon chunks directly into a blender.
  2. Add the fresh lime juice, mint leaves, and sweetener if using.
  3. Pour in a small splash of water or coconut water to help the blender blades catch the frozen fruit.
  4. Blend on high until smooth and slushy. If it's too thick, add a tiny bit more liquid.
  5. Pour into chilled glasses and serve immediately.
